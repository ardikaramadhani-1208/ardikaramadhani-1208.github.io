Workflow from raw data to dashboard

Every step is reproducible from the notebook and files I built.

  1. Collect and review the data

    Loaded Dataset_Warmindo.xlsx (499 rows, 12 columns) and checked data types, summary statistics, missing values, and duplicates. Result: no missing values and no duplicates.

  2. Validate the data

    Converted id, invoice_id, and customer_id from numbers to text so they are never summed, and confirmed the date column is a proper datetime.

  3. Construct new tables

    Built Dim_Tanggal (a 243-day calendar with year, month, and Indonesian weekday names) and Dim_Pelanggan (transaction frequency, total spend, segment) with pandas.

  4. Rule-based customer segmentation

    Loyal: 8+ transactions. Regular: 4-7. Occasional: fewer than 4. The rules are simple and easy to explain to a business owner.

  5. Integrate the data

    Exported three tables (Fact_Penjualan, Dim_Tanggal, Dim_Pelanggan) to one Excel file, Dataset_Warmindo_Bersih.xlsx, as a star schema for Power BI.

  6. Build the Power BI dashboard

    Five KPIs, monthly and daily trends, payment mix, order type, top 10 products, product contribution, segmentation, and the top 10 loyal customers, with slicers for exploration.

Findings and recommendations

Figures are calculated from the cleaned data. Recommendations are suggestions based on observed patterns and have not been tested experimentally.

Monthly sales (IDR million)

Green = highest (July), red = lowest (April).

Portions sold by weekday

Green = highest (Tuesday), red = lowest (Thursday).

Soup noodles carry revenue

Soup noodles (mie kuah) bring in 71% of sales (IDR 6.57M). Soto Betawi (80 portions) and Soto Banjar Limau Kuit (79) lead. Recommendation: keep soto flavors well stocked and build signature bundles around them.

Thursday is the weakest day

Only 105 portions and IDR 0.99M, versus 177 portions on Tuesday. Recommendation: test a Thursday-only promo or bundle.

16 loyal customers are worth a lot

Just 16% of customers generate 27% of sales (about IDR 157K each, versus IDR 47K for Occasional). Recommendation: a points program or a reward on the 8th visit.

53 regular customers can level up

The largest segment, contributing 58% of sales. Many are close to the 8-transaction threshold. Recommendation: nudge them with personalized offers.

Dine-in and delivery are nearly equal

Dine-in 52.7%, delivery 47.3%. Recommendation: treat delivery as a core channel: packaging, delivery-time estimates, and shipping promos.

Payments are mostly digital

About 80% of transactions use QRIS (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, others). Recommendation: keep every QRIS channel active and use e-wallet promotions.
